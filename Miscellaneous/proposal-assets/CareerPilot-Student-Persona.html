Composite Persona

Alex Chen, the Direction-Seeking IT Student

A research synthesis, not a real participant. Built from the project proposal and recurring themes in public student job-search discussions.

Alex Chen

Final-year IT master's student

  • Age 23
  • Location Singapore
  • Targets Software, Data, AI roles
  • Experience Coursework + projects
  • Work pass No sponsorship required
  • Time 6-8 hours per week
  • Tools Job boards, spreadsheets, AI
“Show me which roles fit, what to change, and what to prepare next.”

Goals

  • Focus effort on realistic internships and graduate roles.
  • Translate coursework and projects into credible evidence.
  • Improve interview readiness without losing study time.

Pain Points

  • Too many roles and no clear way to prioritise them.
  • Job descriptions make skill gaps feel larger than they are.
  • Work-pass and sponsorship rules can eliminate suitable roles.
  • Tailoring each resume is repetitive and mentally draining.
  • Low response rates create uncertainty about what works.

Behaviours

  • Saves many vacancies across several job platforms.
  • Uses a master resume, then edits keywords per role.
  • Tries AI tools but checks for invented achievements.

What CareerPilot Must Deliver

  • Explainable recommendations and visible skill gaps.
  • JD-grounded resume suggestions with user approval.
  • Role-specific interview practice and next actions.

Desired Journey

01Define IntentRole, location, graduation and constraints
02PrioritiseRanked roles with evidence and gaps
03TailorTruthful resume improvements for one JD
04PrepareInterview practice and clear next actions
